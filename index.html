The Most Beautiful Gardens In Raleigh NC.

Sarah P. Duke Gardens is a botanical garden near Duke University. Welcome to an experience meant for learning, wellness and cultural inspiration.

Things To Do!

  • Explore the different gardens and seasonal flowers
  • Walk along the 5 miles of garden paths
  • Visit the Culberson Asiatic Arboretum and see the beautiful, Asian-inspired gardens
  • Enjoy a picnic
  • Visit the gardens cafe
  • Try the various family-friendly activities

Activities!

  • Walk and explore the gardens using the 5 mile long path and walkways
  • Participate in citizen science projects
  • Indentify plants, animals or fungi
